## 4. Analyst Findings & Behavioral Assessment

1. **Activity Evaluation:**
   User `jdoe` initially attempted to spawn a root bash shell via sudo and was denied. Minutes later, elevated execution occurred reading and modifying `/etc/shadow`.
2. **Access & Compromise Validation:**
   Checksum modification verified on `/etc/shadow`. An unauthorized account or altered hash was injected into the shadow password file.
3. **Analyst Notes & Observations:**
   High probability that user `jdoe`'s credentials were breached or an insider privilege escalation exploit occurred.

---

## 5. False Positive Assessment

- **Known Baseline Triggers:** Legitimate system updates updating package configurations.
- **Verification Result:** Updates are managed through automated configuration tools (Ansible/apt) from root, not interactive `sed` under a standard developer profile. Confirmed **True Positive**.

---

## 6. Containment, Remediation & Recommended Response

- **Immediate Containment:**
  - Isolate `srv-app-01` from the local VLAN.
  - Suspend user account `jdoe` and kill active shell sessions.
- **Eradication & Remediation:**
  - Restore `/etc/shadow` and `/etc/passwd` from immutable verified backup.
  - Audit `/etc/sudoers` and `/etc/sudoers.d/` for backdoor entries.
  - Rotate all system user passwords and SSH keys.

---

## 7. Final Conclusion & Sign-Off

Critical privilege escalation and credential database modification confirmed. Host isolated and incident escalated to forensic triage.
